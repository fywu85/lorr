2. **Resolve the construction budget and state semantics before adding more parameters.** The CGAR prototypes count every examined candidate against the root budget; NMS's construction counter increments on recursive displacement. The same numeric limit therefore means different work. Preserve the primary's live target and recovery regions. Waiting ordinary plans must stay movable. The current construction's unconditional priority acceptance also differs from NMS's non-worsening joint objective. None of these differences is isolated by simply raising a single budget.

3. **Make complete candidate evaluation cheap, then use fixed parallel work.** Precompute legal five-action geometry by cell and orientation, share the immutable geometry and distance tables, and materialize only selected plans. First prove identical decisions at the existing candidate limit and measure the saved time. Then test fixed candidate/repair counts and independent seeded workers on a reserved 16-physical-core allocation. Every worker must finish its prescribed work; choose the completed plan by a fixed score and deterministic tie break. Any deadline overrun remains an explicit `Timeout`. Measure total process memory, including all workers, against 32 GB.

A candidate should beat the cache control across the six full runs, sustain at least the control's last-1,000-step completion rate, and pass primary/recovery/intent-protection regressions. Inspect safety cancellations and commitment expiry as integration diagnostics. Do not claim starvation freedom from finite tests, or infer that assignment cannot improve from the ratio of pickup distance to delay.
